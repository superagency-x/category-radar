    Channels in this snapshot

    Method

    Shelf
    Each market's dominant price-comparison engine (or its leading retailer), category page sorted by popularity. List position is treated as popularity rank.
    Visibility share
    Brand share of the shelf, weighted 1/log₂(rank+1): rank 1 counts 1.0, rank 3 counts 0.5, rank 15 counts 0.25.
    Share of voice
    Brand share of all user ratings on the shelf, a proxy for installed base and historic sales.
    Price index
    Brand median price ÷ market median price × 100, using the lowest offer price per product and ECB reference rates.
    Price tiers
    Percentiles within each market: Entry ≤ P25 < Mainstream ≤ P60 < Premium ≤ P90 < Super-premium.
    Claims
    Multilingual (DE/PL/CS/HU) regex taxonomy run over titles, spec lines and retailer copy. Defined in the category YAML.
    Feature lift
    Share of top-20 products carrying a claim ÷ share of all products carrying it.
    Market needs
    Review texts from the top products' structured data (schema.org), tagged with a multilingual needs lexicon. Salience = % of reviews mentioning the need.
    Ethics
    Public pages only, robots.txt respected, ≥4 s between requests per host, no logins, no CAPTCHA or bot-wall circumvention. Blocked channels are reported, not forced.
